F&B Stories Uploader

A private, single-user tool. Not offered to the public.

F&B Stories Uploader is a private tool operated by Edvyn Tanu. It uploads my own original short videos about food and drink history to my own YouTube channel: youtube.com/channel/UCYSNc72tTCzn4qNhDA6eQSA.

How it works. The videos are produced with AI tools (script, illustrations and voiceover). Every video is reviewed and approved by me before upload. After my approval, my self-hosted assistant runs the upload through the YouTube Data API, using only my own Google account.

Permissions used

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish my videos to my channel.
  • View my YouTube account (youtube.readonly): only to confirm which channel it is connected to.

The app does not access or store data about any other YouTube user.

Uses YouTube API Services. Videos are published to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy.
